# Manta + Manabí Hive Content and Category Map

**Status:** Initial taxonomy based on existing Facebook-group and YouTube-channel topic research

This map keeps the validated subject areas from the earlier audience research while organizing them for the NooBeehood brand. It is a content model, not a technical schema.

## Launch category groups

### 1. Food, shopping, and dining

- Groceries and supermarkets
- Public markets
- Specialty, imported, dietary, and hard-to-find products
- Local substitutes for familiar products
- Restaurants, cafés, bakeries, and bars
- Shopping centers and local shops
- Delivery and prepared food
- Household and personal supplies

Common Scout and newcomer questions:

- Where can I buy a specific product?
- What local substitute should I try?
- Where can I eat with a dietary restriction?
- Which market or store is best for this need?

### 2. Healthcare and insurance

- Hospitals, clinics, and emergency care
- Doctors, dentists, specialists, and therapists
- Pharmacies, medications, and laboratories
- Public and private healthcare orientation
- Health, travel, property, vehicle, and other insurance help
- Languages, accessibility, and appointment processes
- Emergency contacts and preparation

Important details should distinguish availability from a recommendation and show when information was last checked.

### 3. Housing and household services

- Exploratory and short-term stays
- Long-term rentals
- Condos, homes, and land for sale
- Neighborhood and building context
- Real-estate agents and property managers
- Independent attorneys and inspectors
- Contractors, electricians, plumbers, cleaners, and pest control
- Movers, utilities, internet, water, and electricity
- Property oversight for part-time residents
- Pet policies, accessibility, parking, furnishings, and backup utilities

### 4. Transportation, travel, and experiences

- Manta and Guayaquil airport transfers
- Local taxis, drivers, buses, and terminals
- Vehicle ownership, licensing, and driving orientation
- Tours, fishing, sailing, and whale watching
- Travel agencies and trip planning
- Beaches, day trips, and destinations across Manabí
- Accessibility, languages, capacity, pickup, and seasonal availability
- Events, entertainment, concerts, movies, sports, and recurring activities

## Cross-category guide topics

These topics often span several directory categories and should become practical guides, checklists, videos, Q&A sessions, or events.

### Scout — just looking

- Is Manta or Manabí a fit for me?
- What does ordinary daily life feel like?
- Neighborhood reality and exploratory-trip planning
- Real resident budgets and routines
- Healthcare, housing, transportation, climate, and community trade-offs
- What to test before making a decision

### Relocation roadmap

- Twelve months, six months, ninety days, arrival week, and first month
- Visa-path orientation and document preparation
- Apostilles, translations, and identity documents
- U.S. and Canadian planning differences
- Dependents, retirement, work, study, and family considerations

### First 72 hours and first 30 days

- Airport arrival and onward transportation
- Temporary housing
- SIM, internet, money, and banking basics
- Groceries, medications, and immediate healthcare
- Emergency contacts
- Utilities and household setup
- Where and how to ask for help

### Money and administration

- Banking and transfers
- Taxes and tax residency questions
- Pensions and home-country accounts
- Insurance
- Estate and cross-border planning questions
- Bills, utilities, and recurring payments

### Moving logistics

- Pets
- Household goods and shipping
- Prescriptions and medical records
- Phones and internet
- Driving and vehicles
- Document translations
- Timing traps and arrival checklists

### Safety and readiness

- Practical personal and property habits
- Emergency contacts and plans
- Official notices and confirmed local information
- Health and medication preparation
- Avoiding rumor amplification and live-location sharing

### Belonging and local integration

- Newcomer gatherings
- Interest groups
- Spanish learning and practice
- Ecuadorian-led local orientation
- Volunteering and civic participation
- Local customs and respectful expectations
- Resident stories and mentoring

## Search intents to support

| Intent | Example question | Useful result |
|---|---|---|
| Find an item | “Where can I buy gluten-free flour?” | Place, item or substitute, location, contact, last-checked date |
| Find medication | “Which pharmacy may carry this prescription?” | Pharmacy contact, last-reported availability, reminder to confirm directly |
| Choose a place | “Where can I find good seafood?” | Cuisine, location, hours, accessibility, recent details |
| Find a professional | “Is there an English-speaking dentist?” | Specialty, credentials where checkable, languages, contact, last-checked date |
| Find a home | “What long-term rentals allow pets?” | Purpose, location, price period, features, source, availability date |
| Book transportation | “Who offers GYE airport pickup?” | Route, vehicle, capacity, contact, languages, current details |
| Find an experience | “Are there whale-watching trips?” | Operator, season, itinerary, pickup, capacity, contact |
| Find an event | “What is happening this weekend?” | Event, date, venue, location, ticket source, accessibility notes |
| Ask the hive | “I cannot find this—does anyone know?” | A clear request that can become a reusable answer |

## Taxonomy rule

Start with these four broad launch groups and add narrower labels only when real content or repeated searches need them. A category with no useful answers does not help the hive.
