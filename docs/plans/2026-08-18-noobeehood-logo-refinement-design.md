# NooBeehood Logo Refinement Design

**Status:** Approved for implementation  
**Date:** 2026-08-18

## Selected foundation

Concept 3, **Forward Path**, remains the basis of the identity. Its useful idea is forward movement; its literal bee details and decorative motion graphics are not retained.

## Approved refinements

- Keep the bee immediately recognizable, but heavily simplify it.
- Retain only a subtle forward tilt.
- Remove the flight path, underline, antenna dots, stinger, and other character-like details.
- Build the symbol from a compact body, two wings, and only the minimum markings needed to read as a bee.
- Use one uninterrupted charcoal wordmark.
- Distinguish `NooBee` from `hood` by weight only, not color or size.
- Preserve the approved gold, cream, and charcoal palette.

## Review set

Produce three controlled variations rather than unrelated directions:

1. **Balanced Flight** — the clearest bee silhouette and most neutral proportions.
2. **Nested N** — a stronger `N` relationship integrated into the body geometry.
3. **Open Cell** — a more compact hexagonal construction suited to an app icon.

All three use the same wordmark treatment so the review isolates symbol geometry.

## Success criteria

- Reads as a bee without antennae, face, stinger, or flight trail.
- Feels warm and geometric rather than cartoonish.
- Forward tilt is visible but restrained.
- Remains coherent in monochrome and at small sizes.
- Wordmark reads as one name with no overlap or color split.
