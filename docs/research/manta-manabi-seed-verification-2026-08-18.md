# Manta + Manabí initial listing verification

**Verification date:** 2026-08-18  
**Scope:** Task 2 only. This is an evidence ledger, not seed data, an endorsement, or a recommendation. The queue began with the twelve candidates named in the implementation plan. Rejected candidates remain in the ledger, and every replacement attempt is recorded in its original category.

## Evidence standard and method

`source_checked` means the cited public page or public registry result was fetched and the passage below was checked on 2026-08-18. A live provider page alone is not registration evidence. A failed fetch, missing Manta evidence, or missing applicable current registration causes rejection rather than a weaker claim. Quotes are reproduced in the source language. No claim is made about languages, accessibility, pet policies, availability, routes, seasons, price, quality, safety, or endorsement.

For the ARCSA public registry, the method was an HTTP POST to its public `reportepf.php` endpoint with `tipo_permiso=vigentes`, `r_tipobusqueda=razonsocial`, and the stated search term. For the MINTUR catastro, the method was to download the current national workbook linked by the official catastro page and inspect the matching row. These methods matter because neither application exposes a stable result URL.
