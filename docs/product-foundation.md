# NooBeehood Product Foundation

**Status:** Initial product brief

**First hive:** Manta + Manabí, Ecuador

## Product vision

Create an app where a **NooBee** can quickly answer:

- Where can I find what I need?
- What should I know before moving or arriving?
- Who can help locally?
- What is happening nearby?
- How can I meet people and contribute?

NooBeehood turns scattered questions and answers from Facebook groups, YouTube audiences, local residents, and newcomers into shared hive knowledge that is organized, useful, and kept current.

## The first hive

**Manta + Manabí Hive** is the first local community. Manta is the launch center, while the hive can include relevant places, services, experiences, and contributors across Manabí.

A future hive should be added only when it has local contributors and enough useful information to support newcomers well.

## Audience

| Stage | What the NooBee needs |
|---|---|
| **Scout — “just looking”** | Honest local context, costs, neighborhoods, healthcare, daily-life expectations, and a low-pressure way to explore whether the hive fits |
| **Deciding** | Relocation paths, housing choices, timelines, and trusted questions to ask |
| **Preparing** | Documents, pets, shipping, prescriptions, banking, flights, and temporary housing |
| **Arriving** | Transportation, SIM, money, groceries, healthcare, emergency contacts, and immediate help |
| **Settling** | Homes, services, Spanish, utilities, driving, social life, and reliable referrals |
| **Part-time resident** | Property oversight, bills, maintenance, security, and seasonal planning |
| **Established neighbor** | A simple way to share knowledge, correct details, welcome people, and build the hive |

### The Scout stage

A **Scout** is welcome before they have decided to move. The name connects the user journey to scout bees, which explore beyond the hive and return with information about promising places and resources.

Scout experiences should help a NooBee:

- browse without committing or creating a relocation plan;
- understand daily life through neighborhood, cost, housing, healthcare, and community context;
- compare possibilities and save useful finds;
- follow Manta + Manabí stories, guides, and events from afar;
- ask exploratory questions without being treated as a confirmed mover;
- share useful discoveries back with the hive.

The tone is curious and low pressure: **“Just looking? Scout the neighborhood.”**

## Core product areas

### Discover

One search experience for products, food, stores, restaurants, healthcare, professionals, household services, transportation, tours, entertainment, insurance, and other local needs.

A useful result should say what is available, where it is, how to contact the provider, when the details were last checked, and how to submit a correction.

### Homes

Short stays, long-term rentals, homes and condos for sale, land, property managers, inspectors, attorneys, contractors, movers, utilities, and property-care services.

### Guides

Practical, stage-based local and relocation information. Guides should answer the question first, use specific dates, identify their source, and distinguish general orientation from personal professional advice.

### Events

Meetups, tours, classes, markets, cultural activities, entertainment, sports, and recurring local experiences.

### Community

Connections to the existing Facebook groups, YouTube channel, Q&A sessions, newcomer gatherings, local organizations, and contribution opportunities.

## Core hive-building loop

```text
NooBee searches or asks
        ↓
The hive offers an answer or identifies a gap
        ↓
A resident, contributor, or provider shares useful information
        ↓
The information is checked, organized, and published
        ↓
Future NooBees find a better answer
```

The app should make these actions obvious:

- Search the hive.
- Ask the hive.
- Suggest a place or service.
- Correct an answer.
- Share local knowledge.
- Join a gathering.

## Highest-priority needs

The existing audience and topic research supports this order:

1. **Local discovery:** “Where can I find X?”
2. **A current relocation roadmap.**
3. **Accommodations and real estate.**
4. **Trusted local referrals.**
5. **Healthcare and insurance navigation.**
6. **Visa and document workflow.**
7. **Practical safety information.**
8. **Money, tax, and cross-border administration.**
9. **Moving and arrival logistics.**
10. **Belonging and local integration.**

## Initial useful experiences

1. Search the Manta + Manabí Hive.
2. Ask when a search has no useful result.
3. First 72 hours and first 30 days.
4. “Is Manta right for me?”
5. Food and specialty-product finder.
6. Healthcare provider finder by specialty and language.
7. Short-term stays, rentals, and property discovery.
8. Airport transfers and local transportation.
9. Tours, entertainment, and events.
10. Newcomer coffee, orientation, and live Q&A.

## Content principles

- Answer the question in the first paragraph.
- Use specific dates instead of “currently.”
- Show when important details were last checked.
- Separate sourced facts, professional interpretation, and resident experience.
- Include trade-offs and common failure cases.
- Invite corrections and make meaningful corrections visible.
- Do not publish private-group posts, names, or personal details without permission.
- Include Ecuadorian residents, businesses, professionals, and organizations as hive builders.
- Encourage Spanish learning, local participation, and respect for the community.

## Success

The first hive is working when NooBees can find useful answers without repeatedly asking an administrator, corrections improve the shared information, local contributors return, and community connections happen both online and in person.

Useful measures include:

- successful searches;
- common unanswered searches;
- time from question to useful answer;
- corrected or refreshed information;
- returning contributors;
- event attendance and return visits;
- connections to appropriate local help.

## Deliberately undecided

This foundation does not choose a technical stack, hosting model, database, application architecture, or implementation sequence. Those decisions will follow the brand and initial product definition.
